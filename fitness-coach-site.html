SET 02 — ABOUT

Riley Voss, Head Coach

I spent six years coaching Division I track & field before moving into private strength coaching — because the athletes who needed the most individual attention were the ones getting the least of it.

Every program I write starts from an assessment, not a template. If you're a field athlete, your off-season looks nothing like a powerlifter's peaking block, and your coaching shouldn't either.

SET 03 — APPROACH

Three phases, one program.

Every athlete moves through the same three pillars — the mix shifts depending on your sport, your calendar, and where you're starting from.

01 — BUILD

Strength

Progressive, individualized loading built off your assessment — not a generic 5x5. We chase the lifts that carry over to your sport.

02 — SHARPEN

Conditioning

Energy-system work programmed to match your competition demands, so you're not gassed in the moments that matter.

03 — SUSTAIN

Recovery

Mobility, sleep, and load-management built into the plan from week one — so progress doesn't stall out from overuse.
